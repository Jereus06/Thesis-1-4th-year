# APPENDIX A SYSTEM EVALUATION QUESTIONNAIRE

This client questionnaire matches stockcast-client-survey-v1 in the system. The owner or manager and staff rate only features used during assigned tasks: 1 Strongly disagree, 2 Disagree, 3 Neither agree nor disagree, 4 Agree, or 5 Strongly agree. Unused functions may be left unanswered or marked not applicable. Such answers are excluded from rating means. Technical reviewers use the separate criteria below.

### Client Survey: Functional Suitability

1. The product, sales, and stock features support the retail tasks I need to complete.

2. The records shown by the system provide the information I need for my daily work.

3. The forecasting and restocking information is useful for decisions I am authorized to make.

### Client Survey: Reliability

4. The system works consistently when I perform my usual tasks.

5. When an action fails, the system gives enough information for me to recover or retry.

6. My saved records are available when I return to the system.

### Client Survey: Interaction Capability

7. I can find the features I need through the system's navigation.

8. The labels, instructions, and forms make the required steps clear.

9. I can complete my daily tasks without unnecessary difficulty.

### Client Survey: Perceived Performance Efficiency

10. Pages and actions respond quickly enough for my normal work.

11. Saving and retrieving records takes an acceptable amount of time.

12. Forecasting and restocking information is available in time for my work.

### Separate Technical Review: Maintainability

Module responsibilities — Review whether interface, API, data-access, and forecasting modules have identifiable responsibilities and documented inputs and outputs. Record source references and observations.

Controlled change — Document one representative maintenance change, affected modules, and regression checks. Assess whether the change requires unrelated modifications and explain the evidence.

Documentation and verification — Review setup instructions, schema history, API contracts, and relevant checks. Record whether another developer can reproduce the workflow and identify any correction needed.
