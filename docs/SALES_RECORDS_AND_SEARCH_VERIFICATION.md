used read-only database transactions and changed no business records. The imported filename
identifies the supplied simulated file; this audit is not a client research finding.

## Interface changes

- Other uses muted blue (`#7BA3AE`), matching its pie slice, connector and legend swatch.
- Pie percentages appear outside the circle with connectors. Labels are spaced on each side
  to avoid overlaps and leave room for percentage text on mobile. Percentages round to two
  decimals; tiny positive values display `<0.01%`.
- Record a sale has case-insensitive name/SKU search. Explicit product selection, stock/date
  checks and submission retry behavior remain intact. Searching keeps the selected product
  visible, including when it falls outside the search, and Enter in search focuses the
  product selector instead of submitting. Reopening clears the search.
- Sales report uses Sales records and Estimated value per record labels. Its product table
  explains that an imported daily total is one record.
